# Rrrivo Global Trade 🌍

Rrrivo Global Trade is a modern and responsive agriculture trading and e-commerce website designed to connect farmers, suppliers, and customers through a reliable digital marketplace. The platform focuses on delivering fresh, high-quality agricultural products including vegetables, fruits, spices, food grains, oil seeds, and pulses directly from trusted sources.

The website is built with a professional and user-friendly interface to provide a seamless browsing and shopping experience across all devices.

---

# 🚀 Features

- Modern and professional agriculture-themed design
- Fully responsive and mobile-friendly layout
- Sticky navigation bar
- Product categories with subcategories
- Smooth scrolling and modern animations
- Attractive product cards with images, ratings, and pricing
- Search and category filtering system
- Contact form with business details
- Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages
- SEO-friendly website structure
- Fast-loading and optimized performance
