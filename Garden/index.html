Company Profile

Export Experience Built On Quality

RR RIVO GLOBAL TRADE is based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and serves buyers across domestic and international agricultural markets.

01

Company Profile

RR RIVO GLOBAL TRADE is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Under the headship of Mr. Chhajo Lal Saini, MD & CEO, the organization has been scaling new heights in fresh fruits, fresh vegetables, leafy vegetables, and frozen vegetables.

02

Quality Management

Our fruits and vegetables are checked by quality inspectors in compliance with India's strict export standards before supply. With strong experience in regional and seasonal climatic variations, we select top-quality produce from each area according to international requirements.

Our agricultural and market specialists focus on optimal picking time and packing requirements for our clients' countries.

03

Infrastructure

We have state-of-the-art infrastructure equipped with the necessary equipment to carry out operations efficiently. Our facilities support grading, sorting, pre-cooling, cold storage, and export supply for export and local market requirements.

We also own a pack house in the export building at APMC vegetable market.

04

Clientele

We have developed a large clientele network across the globe. Major export destinations include London, Singapore, U.S.A., Dubai, Vietnam, and Malaysia. Customer satisfaction reflects our quality standards and reliability.

05

Quality

The reason we have become a reliable name in India and abroad is our strict emphasis on food product quality. We are highly conscious about health and offer pesticide-free fresh vegetables and fruits.

Our products conform to international quality norms and are carefully tested before being sent to clients. They are full of natural minerals, vitamins, and proteins, with the delicious taste Mother Nature offers them.
